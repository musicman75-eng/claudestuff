# Working in this repo

## Design preferences (Richie)

- **Never use Anton.** Not for titles, captions, or anything else. This overrides any
  general instruction that lists Anton as a preferred font.
- Otherwise: clean modern sans or condensed athletic type (Nimbus Sans Extended,
  Blinker), editorial layouts, high contrast. Palette: warm neutrals, deep navy,
  charcoal, off-white, warm gold, muted teal.
